# Recipe Planning

This context describes saved recipes and the information needed both to shop for and prepare them.

## Language

**Recipe**:
A saved dish with one or more ingredients and at most one optional method.
_Avoid_: Regular list

**Method**:
An optional ordered sequence of method steps for preparing a recipe, distinct from the ingredients it requires.
_Avoid_: Directions, cooking instructions, recipe text

**Method step**:
One instruction within a method. Its position in the method defines when it is performed.
_Avoid_: Direction, instruction item
